# Employee notice — template (EN)

*Template for organizations deploying Shadow AI Sentinel. Adapt the bracketed parts.
The extension shows an equivalent notice once, on first use.*

[Organization] has deployed the **Shadow AI Sentinel** browser extension to help prevent accidental
submission of sensitive data (API keys, secrets, IBAN, card numbers, personal data) to public AI
services such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Le Chat, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Grok and Copilot.

## What the extension does

- Analyzes what you type and the files you attach (text, code, PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX) **locally, in your
  browser**, before they are sent.
- May **block** the message or **replace** sensitive values with tokens (`[REDACTED_…]`).

## What is not collected

- The extension makes **no network connection**. Nothing — neither the content of your messages
  nor any metadata — is sent to [Organization] or to the extension's authors.
- Only local counters (number of blocks/redactions) are stored in your browser.
