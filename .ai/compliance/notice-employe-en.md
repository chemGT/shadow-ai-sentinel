# Employee notice — Shadow AI Sentinel (EN)

**Draft version — to be customized by the employer**

Your organization has deployed the **Shadow AI Sentinel** browser extension to help prevent accidental submission of sensitive data to public AI services (pilot: Claude).

## What the extension does

- **Locally** analyzes text you type before it is sent.
- May **block** submission or **replace** some data with tokens (`[REDACTED_…]`).

## What is not collected

- The **content** of your messages and prompts is **not** sent to a server.

## What may be shared with your organization

**Technical metadata** only, for example:

- type of data detected (API key, IBAN, etc.);
- action taken (block or redact);
- timestamp;
- anonymous technical installation identifier.

## Your rights

Contact your employer’s **security team** or **DPO** for questions or to exercise your rights.

*This notice is shown once on first use of the extension.*
