## Detailed description

**Shadow AI Sentinel** watches what you paste into public AI chatbots and stops sensitive data before it ever leaves your browser.

Everything runs **locally on your device**. In the free tier, no account, no sign-up, and no network connection are required — your text is never sent anywhere.

### What it catches
- 🔑 **API keys & tokens** (AWS, OpenAI, GitHub, Stripe, generic secrets…)
- 🧩 **Generic high-entropy secrets** pasted by accident

### How it protects you
- 🛑 **Block** — stops the message and warns you before it's sent
- ✂️ **Redact** — masks the sensitive value so you can keep chatting safely

Works on **Claude (claude.ai)**, **ChatGPT (chatgpt.com)** and **Gemini (gemini.google.com)**.

### Private by design
- 100% local detection — your prompts never leave your machine on the free tier
- No account, no tracking, no ads
- Open, minimal permissions (only the three AI sites above)
