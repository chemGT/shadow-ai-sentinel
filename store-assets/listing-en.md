## Detailed description

**Shadow AI Sentinel** watches what you paste into public AI chatbots and stops sensitive data before it ever leaves your browser.

Everything runs **locally on your device**. In the free tier, no account, no sign-up, and no network connection are required — your text is never sent anywhere.

### What it catches
- 🔑 **API keys & tokens** (AWS, OpenAI, GitHub, Stripe, generic secrets…)
- 🧩 **Generic high-entropy secrets** pasted by accident

### How it protects you
- 🛑 **Block** — stops the message and warns you before it's sent
- ✂️ **Redact** — masks the sensitive value so you can keep chatting safely

Works on **Claude** (claude.ai), **ChatGPT** (chatgpt.com), **Gemini** (gemini.google.com), **Mistral Le Chat** (chat.mistral.ai), **DeepSeek** (chat.deepseek.com), **Perplexity** (perplexity.ai), **Grok** (grok.com) and **Microsoft Copilot** (copilot.microsoft.com).

### Private by design
- 100% local detection — your prompts never leave your machine on the free tier
- No account, no tracking, no ads
- Open, minimal permissions (only the eight AI sites above)

## ⭐ Go Pro (subscription)
Unlock advanced protection with a Pro license key:

- 🪪 **Personal data (PII) detection** — IBAN, phone numbers, work emails, internal IP addresses
- 📎 **Attachment scanning** — catch secrets inside text & code files (.env, .json, .csv, sources…) before upload
- ⚙️ **Allowlists** — tune detection to your team, silence false positives

The license key is verified locally against Lemon Squeezy — the only network call the Pro tier makes.
