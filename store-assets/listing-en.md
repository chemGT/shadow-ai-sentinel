Productivity (secondary: Privacy & Security)

## Detailed description

**Shadow AI Sentinel** watches what you paste into public AI chatbots and stops sensitive data before it ever leaves your browser.

Everything runs **locally on your device**. No account, no sign-up, and no network connection are required — your text is never sent anywhere.

### What it catches
- 🔑 **API keys & tokens** (AWS, OpenAI, GitHub, Stripe, generic secrets…)
- 🧩 **Generic high-entropy secrets** pasted by accident
- 🪪 **Personal data (PII)** — IBAN, payment cards, phone numbers, work emails, internal IP addresses
- 📎 **Attachments** — secrets inside text, code, PDF and DOCX files before upload
- ⚙️ **Allowlists** — silence false positives for your team

### How it protects you
- 🛑 **Block** — stops the message and warns you before it's sent
- ✂️ **Redact** — masks the sensitive value so you can keep chatting safely

Works on **Claude** (claude.ai), **ChatGPT** (chatgpt.com), **Gemini** (gemini.google.com), **Mistral Le Chat** (chat.mistral.ai), **DeepSeek** (chat.deepseek.com), **Perplexity** (perplexity.ai), **Grok** (grok.com) and **Microsoft Copilot** (copilot.microsoft.com).

### Private by design
- 100% local detection — your prompts never leave your machine
- No account, no tracking, no ads
- Free and open source (Apache-2.0) — audit the code yourself: https://github.com/chemGT/shadow-ai-sentinel
- Minimal permissions (only the eight AI sites above)
